PLAN YOUR FURNITURE ORDER

What goes into a
guest room furniture package?

Use this example scope to build your furniture schedule. It is a planning checklist, not a fixed-price package or a completed Maldives project.

Bedroom & storage

  • Bed base and headboard, with mattress dimensions confirmed
  • Bedside tables and any integrated fittings
  • Wardrobe, hangers and internal storage requirements
  • Luggage bench, desk or dressing table and chair
  • TV unit, minibar cabinet and mirror where required

Seating & balcony

  • Armchair or compact sofa and side table
  • Dining or work furniture to match the room layout
  • Balcony table and seating where required
  • Upholstery and finishes selected for each use area
  • Spare hardware and replacement parts, if specified

For repeated rooms, confirm one room schedule and multiply it by each room type. Discuss a sample room or sample pieces before approving a larger production run.

BUDGET & QUOTATION

How much does a hotel
furniture order cost?

A useful estimate starts with quantities and specifications. Room count alone does not define the furniture cost.

What changes the furniture price?

Dimensions, materials, upholstery, hardware, finish, quantities and customization all affect the factory quotation. A bedroom package with standard pieces differs from bespoke cabinetry and premium finishes.

Where suitable, we can compare alternatives and explain which specification changes affect the price.

What should you send us?

Your island and project type; number of rooms by type; drawings or reference images; furniture quantities; approximate furniture budget; and the target delivery date.

Confirm whether the budget covers furniture only or also freight and local costs. Packing, shipping, import charges and island transfer should be identified separately.

HOW IT WORKS

Your project.
Our work on the ground.

You have one point of contact for factory selection, approvals, production updates and shipment preparation.

  1. Share the brief

    Send drawings, a furniture schedule, a BOQ (bill of quantities), or reference images. Tell us the quantities, budget, island and target date.

  2. Compare & approve

    We shortlist factories, compare quotations and coordinate dimensions, finishes and samples. The scope and payment schedule are agreed before ordering.

  3. Coordinate production

    We follow up with factories and report on progress. Any proposed changes are discussed before approval.

  4. Check & consolidate

    We check products against the agreed requirements and inspection scope, share photo and video reports, and coordinate consolidation and packing.

  5. Arrange the shipment

    We coordinate freight to the agreed destination. Your team handles Maldives import clearance; onward island delivery is arranged separately.

BEFORE YOU START

Hotel furniture sourcing:
practical questions.

Can you work with different budgets?

Yes. We source from different manufacturers and compare options against your budget. We explain material and specification differences so you can make an informed choice.

Can you manufacture from our drawings?

We select factories that can produce to your approved drawings, dimensions, material requirements and finishes. Samples or a sample room can be discussed before a larger order.

What about humidity and outdoor use?

Tell us where each piece will be used: an air-conditioned room, covered terrace or exposed poolside area. We review material, hardware, upholstery and finish options with the factory for the intended use. Specifications and any testing requirements must be confirmed before production.

What are your minimum order and lead time?

They depend on the factory, product and degree of customization. Share your quantities and target opening date so we can check feasibility and prepare a production and freight schedule.

How do payment and contracting work?

We operate through GUANGZHOU MD IMPORT AND EXPORT CO., LTD in China. Bank transfer and other agreed payment arrangements are discussed with the quotation. Currency, payment milestones and bank details are confirmed before payment.

Do you work with local contractors and procurement companies?

Yes. We can support your team with factory sourcing, production follow-up, agreed quality checks and consolidation in China. Your local team can manage the client, import clearance and site work.
