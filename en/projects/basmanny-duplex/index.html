Furnishing of a duplex apartment in Moscow's Basmanny District. Furniture, lighting, sanitary ware and interior pieces were selected and supplied for each space in a unified style.

Special attention was given to matching materials, tones and proportions with the two-level layout. The scope included pieces for the living room, mezzanine, kitchen, dining area and bathroom.
