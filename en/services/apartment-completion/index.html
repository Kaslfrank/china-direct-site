Planning your order

Can I order without an interior designer?

Yes. Start with a floor plan, dimensions and reference photos. We discuss your furniture list and requirements before approaching factories.

Can I order custom furniture?

Many factories offer custom dimensions, materials and finishes. Factory minimums, samples and drawings are reviewed for your specific order.

Can you ship to my country?

We welcome enquiries from homeowners in the Americas, Europe, Asia and other regions. Send your country and city so we can confirm route availability, product requirements and the proposed delivery scope.

What is included in the quotation?

We identify the furniture specification and the agreed sourcing, inspection, packing and freight scope. Import duties, destination charges, local delivery and installation responsibilities are confirmed for your order.

What are the price and lead time?

They depend on the selected factories, quantities, materials, customization and destination. We review these after receiving your brief and provide a project-specific quotation.
