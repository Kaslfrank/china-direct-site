FAQ

Common questions

Can I come without a complete design project?

Yes. Floor plans, dimensions, references and an indicative budget will still help us build a focused itinerary and make the trip more productive.

Do you work only with clients from Russia?

No. We support private clients, designers and businesses from different countries. The working language and programme details are agreed in advance.

Can I combine several product categories?

Yes. A typical itinerary can combine furniture, lighting, sanitary ware, décor and finishes. The number of directions depends on the trip length and project scale.

What happens after I return home?

We continue managing the order: coordinating details with factories, inspecting production and quality, consolidating goods and arranging international delivery.
