HOTEL DESIGN, SUPPLY & INSTALLATION

Hotel furnishing.
To your design or developed for your building.

China Direct coordinates your project with specialist hotel furnishing manufacturers in China. Work from your existing design or develop a furnishing scheme for your building.

01 / DESIGN & SUPPLY

Starting with your building

Send your floor plans, room dimensions, property concept and budget. Our manufacturing partners can develop an interior furnishing design for your building, with layouts, furniture, finishes and materials agreed before production.

02 / SUPPLY TO YOUR DESIGN

Already have a design?

We coordinate manufacture and procurement to your approved drawings and specifications. Our team helps align dimensions, materials, upholstery and finishes across guest rooms and shared spaces.

Beyond the furniture

Build an agreed package for bedrooms, suites, reception, restaurants and lounges. Alongside custom and freestanding furniture, the scope can include wall panels, lighting, curtains and blinds, rugs and carpets, bedding, bathroom fittings and guest-room accessories.

China Direct remains your point of contact for partner coordination, approvals, production follow-up, agreed inspections and shipment preparation.

OPTIONAL ON-SITE TEAM

Installation at your property

A specialist team may be arranged by separate agreement. For each property, we first confirm with the factory whether an on-site visit is possible. The scope, schedule and travel arrangements are then agreed. Installation labour, flights and accommodation are quoted separately.

What we need from you

Client-supplied plans and measurements are the starting point. Share the property location, number of rooms, site readiness and target opening date. Final dimensions, installation responsibilities and any site checks are confirmed before production and travel.

The quotation defines design deliverables, supplied items, shipping arrangements and any installation work. Installation labour, flights and accommodation are calculated separately.

A COORDINATED PROJECT

From your brief
to the agreed supply.

CHINA DIRECT coordinates factory selection, specifications, production follow-up and shipment preparation.

  1. Share the building plans

    The client provides floor plans, room dimensions, room quantities, design references, budget and target dates.

  2. Agree the design and scope

    Work to your approved project or agree design development for your building. Confirm layouts, materials and specifications before production.

  3. Approve and manufacture

    Review factory options, quotations, samples and drawings. Approve the scope before production begins.

  4. Prepare delivery and site work

    Agree inspections, packing and shipping. If you need an on-site team, we first confirm feasibility with the factory, then agree a separate quotation.
